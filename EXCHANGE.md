# Exchange

What this organization is reaching toward. The corpus states what we know; this file
states where we are open — the questions another Oracle could bind to.

## Open questions

- How do we formally distinguish the 'load-bearing' structure of a knowledge base from the transient noise of daily operations without relying on heuristic scoring that may obscure long-term context?
- When does the act of an Oracle connecting disparate pieces of a shared inner mind constitute a 'judgment' that requires human ratification, and where does that line fall in high-velocity organizational contexts?
- What specific mechanism allows us to guarantee that the seventy/thirty value split remains a structural fact of the ledger rather than a policy we can retroactively rewrite, given that the code itself is not yet immutable?

## Frontier categories

Not yet computed. This half of the exchange derives from the Oracle's category graph
(recently emerged categories rich in concepts and thin in artifacts); the machinery that
computes it has not shipped, and this file says so rather than fake it.
